🗣️ Prompt-Aware Speech Scoring System for Second Language Learners

January 15, 2023

Overview#

Developed an end-to-end automatic speech scoring system for second language learners that addresses the cold-start problem by incorporating prompt (text and image) awareness. The system was successfully deployed to SANTA Say, a TOEIC Speaking practice app.

Key Achievements#

  • Publication: Accepted to InterSpeech 2023 (top conference on audio & signal processing)
  • Production Deployment: Integrated into SANTA Say TOEIC Speaking App
  • Cold-Start Robustness: Achieved reliable performance even with limited training data for new prompts
  • Real-World Impact: Helping thousands of language learners improve their speaking skills

Innovation#

Traditional speech scoring systems struggle when encountering new prompts (questions) they haven’t seen during training. Our system incorporates both text and image information from the prompts, allowing it to:

  • Better understand the context of what learners are responding to
  • Provide more accurate scores even for new, unseen prompts
  • Adapt quickly to new question types

Tech Stack#

  • Deep Learning: Speech processing models
  • Multimodal: Text and image encoders for prompt understanding
  • Deployment: Production-grade serving for mobile app

Impact#

Enables fair and consistent scoring for speaking practice, helping language learners track their progress and identify areas for improvement, even as new practice questions are added to the platform.

Period#

October 2022 - January 2023

Publication#

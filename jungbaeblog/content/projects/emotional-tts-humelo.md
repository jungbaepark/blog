## Overview

As Research Lead and COO at Humelo, I led the development of next-generation emotional Text-to-Speech systems that could convey nuanced emotions and expressions in synthesized speech. This project was funded by the Ministry of SMEs and startups.

## Key Achievements

- **Government Grant**: Secured TIPS R&D grant from Ministry of SMEs (S2644149)
- **Publications**: Multiple papers accepted to **ICASSP** (top conference on audio & signal processing)
- **Multi-speaker**: Developed systems supporting multiple voices and speaking styles
- **Emotional Control**: Fine-grained control over emotional expression in synthesized speech

## Technical Innovations

### 1. Factorized Hierarchical VAE
Developed multi-speaker and multi-domain emotional voice conversion using hierarchical variational autoencoders.

**Publication**: Mohamed Elgaar, *Jungbae Park, *Sang Wan Lee - "Multi-speaker and Multi-domain Emotional Voice Conversion Using Factorized Hierarchical Variational Autoencoder" (ICASSP 2020)

### 2. Phonemic-Level Duration Control
Achieved natural speech synthesis through attention alignment-based duration control.

**Publication**: Jungbae Park et. al. - "Phonemic-level duration control using attention alignment for natural speech synthesis" (ICASSP 2019, Oral)

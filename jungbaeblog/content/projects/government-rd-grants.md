## Overview

As COO and Research Lead at Humelo (휴멜로), I planned, proposed, and led multiple government and public R&D projects that provided critical funding for the company's AI speech technology research. Over two years, I secured approximately US$875K in total funding across three competitive grants from Korean government agencies, covering research areas including brain-inspired AI, emotional text-to-speech synthesis, and voice conversion technology.

## Key Achievements

- **Total Funding Secured**: Approximately **US$875K** across three government R&D grants
- **IITP Brain-Inspired AI Grant** (No. 2019-0-01371): Co-PI on a research team grant for the Development of Brain-Inspired AI, funded at approximately **US$225K** (2019-2020)
- **Ministry of SMEs TIPS R&D Grant** (S2644149): Leading researcher, co-manager, and co-proposal author for next-generation deep-learning emotion and expression TTS, funded at approximately **US$400K** (2018-2020)
- **Seoul R&BD Program** (CY190019): Leading researcher, co-manager, and co-proposal author for Voice Conversion (VC) and TTS for VC research, funded at approximately **US$250K** (2019-2020)

## Technical Approach

### Grant Strategy and Proposal Writing

Each grant required a distinct strategy tailored to the funding agency's priorities. For the IITP grant, the proposal emphasized fundamental research connecting neuroscience principles to AI architectures. The TIPS grant from the Ministry of SMEs focused on commercial viability and startup growth potential of emotional TTS technology. The Seoul R&BD Program proposal centered on practical applications of voice conversion for the Seoul metropolitan area's technology ecosystem.

### Research Planning and Execution

For each project, I developed detailed research plans with milestones, resource allocation, and deliverable timelines. This involved coordinating across Humelo (휴멜로)'s research and engineering teams, managing relationships with academic collaborators, and ensuring compliance with government reporting requirements. Each grant demanded quarterly progress reports, financial audits, and annual reviews.

### IITP: Brain-Inspired AI (No. 2019-0-01371)

As co-PI, I contributed to research exploring how principles from neuroscience could inform the design of more efficient and capable AI systems. This grant connected Humelo (휴멜로)'s applied speech research with broader fundamental AI research goals.

### TIPS: Emotional TTS (S2644149)

This was the largest grant and most directly aligned with Humelo (휴멜로)'s core product. The research focused on developing deep-learning-based TTS systems capable of conveying nuanced emotions and natural expressions. The work produced multiple publications at top-tier conferences including ICASSP and led to several patent filings.

### Seoul R&BD: Voice Conversion (CY190019)

This project tackled the challenge of converting one speaker's voice to sound like another while preserving linguistic content. The research explored both traditional signal processing approaches and modern deep learning methods, with applications in personalized speech synthesis and content creation.

## Impact

The combined US$875K in funding was essential for Humelo (휴멜로) as an early-stage startup. These grants enabled the company to hire researchers, acquire computational resources, and pursue ambitious research directions that would not have been feasible with private funding alone. The research output from these grants -- including publications at ICASSP, registered patents, and functional prototypes -- established Humelo (휴멜로)'s credibility in the AI speech technology space and contributed to the company winning the Minister of Science and ICT Special Award at K-Startup 2018.

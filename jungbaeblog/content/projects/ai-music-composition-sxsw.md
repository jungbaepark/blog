## Overview

Led the development of AI systems for music composition and arrangement as part of "Play with Error" and later Humelo. This innovative project combined deep learning with music theory to create AI-aided music, culminating in collaborations with major K-pop artists and presentation at SXSW 2019.

## Key Achievements

- **SXSW 2019**: Presented AI-aided music production at South by Southwest in Austin, Texas
- **SM Entertainment Collaboration**: Worked with SM Entertainment through KOCCA's "Music X A.I." program
- **Artist Collaborations**: Created AI-generated music with Korean hip-hop band XXX, rapper Sleepy, and other artists
- **Media Coverage**: Featured on KBS1 Documentary "New-contents, changing the world"
- **Patents**: Multiple registered patents for polyphonic music generation systems

## Projects

### 1. Alphach - Composing AI
Developed an AI system capable of:
- Generating chord progressions
- Creating melodic compositions
- Polyphonic music generation based on LSTM and polynomial approaches

### 2. Rapper AI
Created an AI that could:
- Generate rap lyrics
- Collaborate with human rappers
- Featured in collaboration with rapper Sleepy

### 3. AI-Aided Music Production
**SXSW 2019 Showcase**: "XXX – Flight Attendant"
- Integrated sound and visual interpretation
- Collaborated with XXX (Korean Hip-hop Band), Seonggu de Kim (Sound Designer, DJ), and Seong Pil Kim (Visual Artist)
